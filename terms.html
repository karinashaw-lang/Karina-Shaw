What Groundtruth is

Groundtruth assembles self-help legal documents from a library of pre-written clauses, each tied to the law it's based on and when that was last checked. It is a document generator and information tool, not a lawyer. Using it does not create an attorney-client relationship with anyone, and nothing it generates, displays, or says about a document you paste in is legal advice. It describes what a clause says and what law it cites, or what a pasted passage resembles in its library — never whether a document is right for your situation, whether it will be enforced the way you want, or what you should do.

No guarantee of legal effect

We do not guarantee that a document you generate here is legally sufficient, complete, enforceable, or appropriate for your situation or jurisdiction. Laws change, and a clause's "last checked" date tells you when it was last verified against the cited law, not that it is current today. For anything with real stakes, consult a licensed attorney.

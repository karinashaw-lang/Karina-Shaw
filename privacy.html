Groundtruth is a document-assembly tool. Most of what it does happens entirely in your browser, with no account and no data sent anywhere. This page describes the small amount of information involved when you do create an account, submit feedback, or use the sharing/signing feature.

What stays only in your browser

Filling out a document, saving it to your library, and tracking "promises" all work with no account at all, stored in your browser's own localStorage. Nothing about this reaches Groundtruth or anyone else unless you choose to create an account and sync. See PRIVACY_DATA_INVENTORY.md for the exact list of what's stored this way and how to delete each piece.

What we collect if you create an account

An account needs only an email address, used to send you a one-time sign-in link — there is no password to set or to be compromised elsewhere. Supabase, the database and authentication service this project runs on, stores that email address and basic session metadata (when you signed in, your session's expiry) in its own auth.users table.

Signing in additionally mirrors your saved documents, their version history, and a record of each document you generate (which clauses it used and when, never your answers) to the database, so they sync across devices and so you have an audit trail. The full table-by-table list — every column, who can read it, and how it's deleted — is in PRIVACY_DATA_INVENTORY.md.

What we collect without an account

  • Usage analytics (which step of a wizard was reached, which document was opened or exported, what was searched) — carries no name, email, or account link of any kind, and no IP address. Turned off entirely from the Account screen's analytics toggle.
  • Error reports (a JavaScript error and its stack trace) — scrubbed in your browser before it's ever sent, to strip anything that looks like an email address or a long quoted value, before it leaves your device.
  • Feedback you type into the in-app feedback form — usable without signing in.
  • A signature, if someone signs a document you share with them — their typed or drawn signature and the IP address they signed from, visible only to the document's owner.

What we never collect

  • No cookies of any kind (see the Cookie/Storage notice).
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking script.
  • No IP address stored anywhere except the one signature case above, and a short-lived internal counter that exists only to slow down abusive request bursts.
  • We do not sell personal information, and do not share it with third parties for advertising.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

The Account screen has two self-service tools that cover most of this: "export all my data" (every row associated with your account, in one file) and "delete my account" (which removes your account and, through the database's own cascading-delete rules, every row tied to it — documents, versions, audit entries, shares, and signatures on shares you created). Analytics events, error reports, and anonymously submitted feedback carry no identifier connecting them back to you in the first place, so there is nothing to locate by request there — not because it is deleted on request, but because it was never tied to an identifiable person to begin with. Full retention detail, table by table, is in PRIVACY_DATA_INVENTORY.md.

Your California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the categories below describe how each right maps onto the real tools this product has today — not a generic list copied from elsewhere.

  • Right to know / access — the Account screen's "export all my data" gives you everything tied to your account, in one request, with no waiting period.
  • Right to delete — the Account screen's "delete my account" removes your account and everything tied to it, immediately. As above, analytics/error/anonymous feedback data has no identifier to delete by, since it was never linked to you.
  • Right to correct — you can edit and regenerate any document in your library yourself at any time. There is currently no separate tool to edit a past audit-log entry or a signature record (both exist specifically as a historical record of what was generated or signed at the time, which correcting after the fact would undermine the purpose of); contact us if one of those needs correcting for a specific reason.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing — we do not sell or share personal information with third parties, so there is nothing to opt out of in that sense. The separate analytics toggle on the Account screen controls whether usage events are recorded at all, which is a different thing and is on by default.
  • Non-discrimination — exercising any of the above never changes what the product costs or what it does for you.

Children's privacy

Groundtruth is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Groundtruth does not use cookies

Nothing on this site sets a cookie — not for tracking, not for preferences, not for anything else. Everything below is localStorage or sessionStorage, which work differently from cookies in one important way: they are never sent to a server automatically. A site can only read its own storage when you're actually on that site, and nothing here is shared with any other site.

What's stored in localStorage

  • groundtruth-draft — your in-progress answers for whichever document you're currently filling out.
  • groundtruth-library — every document you've saved, with its answers and version history.
  • groundtruth-promises — the free-text list you've typed into the "Promises" tracker.
  • groundtruth-analytics-opt-out — whether you've turned off analytics from the Account screen.
  • sb-<project-ref>-auth-token — if you sign in, your current login session, written by the Supabase client library itself rather than this project's own code.

All of these persist until you remove them (either from within the app, where each feature has its own delete action, or by clearing this site's data in your browser) or until you clear your browser's storage yourself. None of them are sent anywhere unless the specific feature they back is one that syncs to an account you've created — see the Privacy Policy for which features do.

What's stored in sessionStorage

  • groundtruth-analytics-session — a random id grouping this browser tab's own analytics events together. It is not a persistent identifier: it's generated fresh and disappears the moment you close the tab, and nothing about it lets a later visit be connected back to this one.

The full, current list

The exact list above is generated from the real app code, not maintained separately from it — see PRIVACY_DATA_INVENTORY.md in the project repository for the always-current version, including what's additionally stored in the database if you create an account.

No third-party trackers

This site loads no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking script of any kind. The only third-party network requests it makes at all are to Google Fonts (for the typefaces used on this page) and, for app pages, to Supabase (only if you've created an account and are signed in).

Controlling this

Most browsers let you clear a single site's storage from their settings, or block it entirely for sites you choose. Clearing it here removes your local drafts, library, and promises permanently — anything not also synced to an account cannot be recovered afterward.

PPG Architectural Finishes; Vatalaro v. County of Sacramento; Ververka v. Department of Veterans Affairs; CACI Nos. 4603, 4604) — Information Sheet

The chosen topic is therefore genuinely open, and it is the piece the
existing Ending-employment shelf most obviously lacks: the corpus
already explains *what* § 1102.5 forbids and *how FEHA* mixed-motive
causation works, but nothing in it explains how a § 1102.5 discharge
claim is actually litigated — the two-step statutory framework, the
"contributing factor" standard, the displacement of McDonnell Douglas,
and the employer's clear-and-convincing same-decision burden.

- **`wb1102_6_summary_judgment_application`** — Lawson's rejection of a

- **`wb1102_6_comparable_frameworks_relied_on_in_lawson`** — the
  Mt. Healthy First Amendment framework and the Sarbanes-Oxley
  whistleblower framework as displacing rather than supplementing
  McDonnell Douglas.

## Genuine findings

- **Lawson's reporter pagination is not in any retrievable copy.**
  Every copy of Lawson available without the CourtListener MCP quota
  (the CourtListener opinion page and both slip PDFs) is the slip
  opinion, carrying the court's own 1–20 pagination and no `*NNN`
  official-reporter star pagination. Pin cites into 12 Cal.5th are
  therefore given **only** where a later published decision or the
  Judicial Council pinpoints the quoted words: p. 707 and p. 712
  (Ververka; CACI Nos. 4603 and 4604 Sources and Authority), p. 714
  (Ververka, for the contributing-factor passage), pp. 715–716
  (Vatalaro), p. 718 and p. 718 fn. 2 (Vatalaro; CACI No. 4603
  Directions for Use). The remaining Lawson quotes carry the bare
  citation `12 Cal.5th 703`, and the practice is disclosed in every
  gap field that relies on it. Vatalaro's p. 379 pin cite comes the
  same way, from CACI No. 4604's Sources and Authority. No pin cites
  into 79 Cal.App.5th or 101 Cal.App.5th are given beyond that one.
- **A Lawson sentence is unquotable because a footnote splits it.**
  The sentence introducing the Sarbanes-Oxley comparison ("For
  instance, the federal Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002 … contains a nearly
  identical burden-shifting framework…") straddles slip pages 14–15,
  and footnote 1 sits at the foot of the intervening page, so in every
  fetched copy — HTML and both PDFs alike — the footnote text is
  interpolated mid-sentence. There is no contiguous verbatim string to
  quote. The clause therefore summarizes that sentence and quotes the
  adjacent, contiguous "Much the same is true of courts interpreting
  federal statutes similar to section 1102.6." Disclosed in the gap.
- **Triple-nested quotation marks.** Lawson renders Mt. Healthy's
  "'substantial'" / "'motivating'" and Rookaird's "contributing
  factor" definition inside three layers of quotation marks with
  interior spaces (`" 'substantial' "`). Rather than reproduce or
  silently reflow them, those passages are summarized in the clause
  bodies, and the contributing-factor definition is quoted instead
  from CACI No. 4603's own single-layer text. Disclosed in both gaps.
- **Vatalaro says "burden of production" where the statute says
  "burden of proof."** Vatalaro wrote: "But section 1102.6 explicitly
  imposes a higher burden of production, clear and convincing
  evidence." Section 1102.6's own words assign the employer "the
  burden of proof," and Lawson likewise describes a burden of proof.
  The sentence is quoted exactly as the court wrote it and the
  mismatch is flagged in the gap rather than smoothed over.
- **Ververka was modified after publication.** Filed 5/6/24, certified
  for publication 5/22/24, modified 6/5/24. The modification (cluster
  9511502) deletes one sentence on slip pages 12–13 ending "based on
  nothing more than stray remarks" and replaces it with "…based on
  nothing more than passing remarks unrelated to the disputed
  employment decision." **No sentence quoted in this document is
  touched by that modification** — the quoted passages are the
  introduction, the "not reasonably susceptible" holding, the
  Harris-distinguishing paragraph, and the plain-language reasoning.
  The modification is disclosed in the gap; the unmodified opinion
  (cluster 9506471) is what was fetched and quoted.
- **CACI PDF line-wrap inside bracketed pronoun options.** CACI
  No. 4604's instruction text wraps "[he/she/nonbinary pronoun/it]"
  across lines twice, so the extracted text carries a space after the
  slash. The quote closes that gap; the artifact and the fix are
  disclosed in that clause's gap field, and a check confirmed this is
  the **only** one of the 41 quotes that depends on the slash
  normalization.
- **§ 1102.5's current text post-dates Lawson.** The subdivisions
  quoted are as amended by Stats. 2023, ch. 612 (S.B. 497), effective
  January 1, 2024 — after Lawson was decided. The protected-activity
  language in (b), (c), and (d) is unchanged by that amendment; S.B.
  497's change to this section concerns the subdivision (f) civil
  penalty. § 1102.6 itself has never been amended (Stats. 2003,
  ch. 484, § 3).
- **Ververka and Vatalaro are Court of Appeal decisions from different
  districts** (First District Division One; Third District). Neither
  binds the other Courts of Appeal, and the Supreme Court has not
  addressed whether a § 1102.6 same-decision showing bars all relief.
  Disclosed in both gap fields.

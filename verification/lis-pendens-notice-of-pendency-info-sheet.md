# Real Estate, new document: Notice of Pendency of Action (Lis Pendens), Expungement, and Withdrawal — Information Sheet

Wave 164 (Real Estate). Continues the even round-robin of waves 146,
148, 150, 152, 154, 156, 158, 160, and 162.

## Why this document

Before this document, the corpus's 85 Real Estate documents had no
document on California's lis pendens scheme. A grep of both data files
for `lis pendens`, `pendency of action`, `405.3`, `Kirkeby`, and
`405.2` found:

- `quiettitle_commencement_and_lis_pendens`, a clause inside the Quiet
  Title Action information sheet, which covers the *quiet title*
  statute's own mandatory recording step (Code Civ. Proc. § 761.010,
  subd. (b)) and describes constructive notice in general terms. It
  quotes no provision of Title 4.5 and says nothing about who may sign
  a notice, the mailing and proof-of-service requirements, expungement,
  undertakings, writ review, fees, or withdrawal.
- Two incidental uses of the phrase "pendency of action" in unrelated
  Estate Planning and Family Law clauses.
- **Zero** hits anywhere in the corpus for `405.3`, `405.22`, `405.31`,
  `405.32`, `405.38`, `Kirkeby`, or `probable validity`.

The Quiet Title sheet is cross-referenced rather than duplicated: this
sheet does not restate the quiet title recording requirement, and the
new clause on the effect of recording expressly points to the corpus's
existing California Recording Act and priority information sheet for
the separate priority rules. The eminent domain carve-out in
§ 405.6 is stated and its own statute (§ 1250.150) is left to the
corpus's existing eminent domain / inverse condemnation sheet.

## What this document covers

15 clauses: 2 drafting (purpose declaration, acknowledgment) and 13
authority clauses, 46 verified quotes in all.

- **`lispendens_definitions_and_scope`** (Code Civ. Proc. §§ 405.2,
  405.1, 405.20, 405.5, 405.8; *Kirkeby v. Superior Court* (2004)
  33 Cal.4th 642) — the definitions of "notice of pendency of action"
  and "claimant," who may record and where, what the notice must
  contain, the extension of the title to actions in a United States
  District Court, and the preservation of other remedies.
- **`lispendens_real_property_claim_definition`** (§ 405.4; *Kirkeby*,
  two quotes; *Park 100 Investment Group II, LLC v. Ryan* (2009)
  180 Cal.App.4th 795) — the statutory definition, Kirkeby's holding
  that a fraudulent conveyance claim seeking avoidance affects title,
  and Park 100's published holding that an easement dispute supports a
  notice on the *dominant* tenement.
- **`lispendens_signing_requirement`** (§§ 405.21, 405.6, 405.7) —
  attorney-of-record signature or judicial approval as a recording
  prerequisite, the eminent domain carve-out, and the affirmative
  recording duty imposed on a public agency suing to declare a
  building uninhabitable.
- **`lispendens_mailing_service_and_proof_of_service`** (§ 405.22, two
  quotes; § 405.23) — registered or certified mail before recording,
  the declaration permitted when no address is known, filing with the
  court immediately after recording, service on later-joined adverse
  parties, and § 405.23's party-by-party voiding rule.
- **`lispendens_constructive_notice_and_relation_back`** (§ 405.24) —
  constructive notice to later purchasers and encumbrancers, the
  fictitious-name limitation, and relation back to the recording date.
- **`lispendens_motion_to_expunge_and_burden_of_proof`** (§ 405.30;
  *Kirkeby*; *The Formula Inc. v. Superior Court* (2008)
  168 Cal.App.4th 1455) — who may move, the intervention requirement
  for nonparties, evidence and discovery, the reversal of the ordinary
  burden, and Formula's holding that the scheme does not reach a
  notice of litigation pending in another state's courts.
- **`lispendens_expungement_no_real_property_claim`** (§ 405.31;
  *Kirkeby*, two quotes) — mandatory expungement on the pleading, the
  bar on an undertaking as a condition, and the demurrer-like analysis
  that does not look past the pleading to the claimant's purpose.
- **`lispendens_expungement_probable_validity`** (§§ 405.32, 405.3;
  *Formula*, two quotes; *Kirkeby*) — the preponderance standard, the
  definition of "probable validity," the statutory history of abuse
  and counterbalancing amendments, and the practical effect of a
  recorded notice on marketability.
- **`lispendens_undertakings`** (§§ 405.33 two quotes, 405.34 two
  quotes, 405.37; *Kirkeby*) — expungement secured by an undertaking,
  the inapplicability of the Civil Code § 3387 uniqueness presumption
  except for an owner-occupied single-family dwelling, the reverse
  motion requiring the claimant to post security, the two-element
  recovery showing with intent and probable cause excluded, and
  exoneration or modification.
- **`lispendens_order_effect_writ_review_and_refiling`** (§§ 405.39,
  405.35, 405.36; *Park 100*) — no appeal, the 20-day writ petition
  and the single extension of up to 10 days, the order's delayed
  effectiveness and unrecordability pending review, and the
  leave-of-court requirement before a second notice.
- **`lispendens_attorney_fees_on_the_motion`** (§ 405.38; *Kirkeby*) —
  prevailing-party fees on any motion under the chapter, running in
  either direction, with the two statutory escapes.
- **`lispendens_withdrawal_and_free_transferability`** (§§ 405.50,
  405.60, 405.61) — voluntary withdrawal by acknowledged recorded
  notice, the cancellation of actual and constructive notice and of
  any duty of inquiry, and the Legislature's stated intent of
  "absolute and complete free transferability."
- **`lispendens_litigation_privilege`** (Civ. Code § 47(b)(4);
  *Park 100*, two quotes) — the privilege exception keyed to whether
  the identified action affects title or the right of possession, and
  Park 100's application of it to defeat slander of title and
  interference claims.

## Genuine corrections and findings

- **No quote in this document reproduces a scanning defect.** The
  CourtListener text of *Kirkeby* carries several OCR artifacts in
  passages near the ones quoted — `foil. § 405.4` for `foll. § 405.4`,
  `[wjith` for `[w]ith`, `Code corns.` for `Code coms.`, `Urez Corp v.`
  for `Urez Corp. v.`, and `Moreno, L,` for `Moreno, J.,` in the
  concurrence line. Every one of those passages was deliberately left
  unquoted. The *Formula* text similarly contains `§ 405:20` for
  `§ 405.20` and `inter aha` for `inter alia`; neither passage is
  quoted. Disclosed in the `gap` of
  `lispendens_expungement_no_real_property_claim`.
- **One Kirkeby sentence is split by a reporter page break.** The
  fetched text renders the court's sentence as "the court must engage
  in a demurrer-like" / `*648` / "analysis." Rather than quote across
  the break or silently repair it, the clause quotes the court's
  *other* statements of the same proposition ("review of an
  expungement order under section 405.31 is limited to whether a real
  property claim has been properly pled by the claimant" and "Nowhere
  in the language of section 405.31, or in its legislative history, is
  the court directed to conduct such an examination during its
  demurrer-like analysis"), both of which sit entirely on one page.
  Disclosed in that clause's `gap`.
- **One quote carries a marked elision around a footnote marker.**
  In *Formula*, footnote reference `10` falls inside the sentence
  "Because of a history of abuse of the lis pendens system by third
  party claimants, [10] the statutes were amended . . . ." The
  citation uses the corpus's ` ... ` elision convention at exactly
  that point, and the `lispendens_expungement_probable_validity`
  `gap` says so.
- **Quoted-within-quoted material is labelled, never laundered.**
  Two case quotes are passages the fetched opinion is itself quoting
  from an opinion that was *not* fetched: Kirkeby's one-sentence
  description of a lis pendens (quoting *Urez Corp. v. Superior Court*
  (1987) 190 Cal.App.3d 1141, 1144) and Kirkeby's passage on the
  practical effect of a recorded notice (quoting *La Paglia v.
  Superior Court* (1989) 215 Cal.App.3d 1322, 1326). Both citations
  name the quoted source in the `cite` field, and both clauses'
  `gap` fields state that the underlying opinion was not fetched and
  that the words are reproduced as the fetched opinion reproduced
  them. Kirkeby itself notes that La Paglia was abrogated on another
  ground; the `gap` repeats that.
- **The statutory title never says "lis pendens."** Title 4.5 uses
  "notice of pendency of action" and, in §§ 405.8 and 405.36, "notice
  of pending action." The phrase "lis pendens" enters the statutes
  only through Civil Code § 47(b)(4). Stated in the body and in the
  `lispendens_definitions_and_scope` `gap` so the two vocabularies are
  not conflated.
- **§ 405.21's prohibition has no stated remedy.** The section says a
  notice "shall not be recorded" without a signature or judicial
  approval but does not say what follows if one is; § 405.23's express
  voiding provision is keyed to § 405.22's service requirements, not
  to § 405.21. No fetched source fills that gap, and the
  `lispendens_signing_requirement` `gap` says so rather than
  extrapolating.
- **Park 100's published/unpublished split is disclosed.** Portions of
  that opinion are unpublished; the holding quoted in
  `lispendens_real_property_claim_definition` is from a portion the
  court itself identifies as published, and the `gap` notes the split.

## Method

**Statutes.** Code Civ. Proc. §§ 405, 405.1, 405.2, 405.3, 405.4,
405.5, 405.6, 405.7, 405.8, 405.20, 405.21, 405.22, 405.23, 405.24,
405.30, 405.31, 405.32, 405.33, 405.34, 405.35, 405.36, 405.37,
405.38, 405.39, 405.50, 405.60, and 405.61, and Civ. Code § 47, were
each fetched **twice** from `leginfo.legislature.ca.gov` via curl
through the environment proxy, with two distinct User-Agent strings
(a Chrome/Windows browser UA and a `groundtruth-corpus-verifier/1.0`
UA). All 28 section pairs extracted to byte-identical text. The proxy
reset several connections mid-transfer (`curl (35) Recv failure` and
`SSL_connect: SSL_ERROR_SYSCALL`); every failure was retried inside
the turn until it returned 200, and § 405.22 in particular failed
repeatedly under the Chrome UA before succeeding on a later retry.
A bulk `codes_displayText.xhtml` fetch of Title 4.5 returned a page
with no section text and was discarded in favor of the per-section
fetches, which also match the corpus's per-section URL convention.

**Case law — no CourtListener MCP calls were made.** All case work
went through the public, unauthenticated v4 search API and the public
opinion HTML, via curl through the proxy.

- *Kirkeby v. Superior Court* (2004) 33 Cal.4th 642 — cluster
  **2580992**, located by public search. Fetched **three** times: twice
  at `/opinion/2580992/x/` with two distinct full header sets
  (Chrome/Windows and Safari/macOS, each with
  `Referer: https://www.courtlistener.com/`), and once at the canonical
  slug URL `/opinion/2580992/kirkeby-v-superior-court-of-orange-county/`.
  All three returned 200 and extracted to identical opinion text.
- *Park 100 Investment Group II, LLC v. Ryan* (2009) 180 Cal.App.4th
  795 — cluster **2255930**, same three-fetch treatment, all 200,
  identical text.
- *The Formula Inc. v. Superior Court* (2008) 168 Cal.App.4th 1455 —
  cluster **2285874**, same three-fetch treatment, all 200, identical
  text. The slug was confirmed by fetching
  `/opinion/2285874/the-formula-inc-v-superior-court/` directly and
  getting 200 (the slugless `/opinion/2285874/` returns 404, so the
  slug is not guessed in the citation URLs).
- Requests without a `Referer` header, and many with one, returned the
  empty 202 the task note describes; every 202 was retried in-turn on
  a 20-25 second loop until a 200 arrived.
- **Phrase-level second verification.** Because the three HTML fetches
  of an opinion come from one CourtListener record, each case quote was
  additionally queried against the public search feed as
  `cluster_id:<id> AND "<exact phrase>"`. Results are recorded below.
  These queries hit both the 5/min and the shared 125/day limits
  repeatedly; each was retried inside the turn on a 90-second loop
  until it returned a count.

Justia, FindLaw, Google Scholar, and case.law are egress-blocked and
were not used. The Official Reports pagination for the three opinions
was not independently consulted; pinpoint pages in the `cite` fields
are taken from the star pagination embedded in the CourtListener text.

## Verification

- **All 46 citation quotes programmatically confirmed as exact
  substrings of every fetched copy** — two copies for each statute
  quote, three for each case quote — after benign normalization only
  (NFKC, nbsp, whitespace/line-wrap collapse, curly vs. straight quotes
  and apostrophes, en/em dash). The checker was run in a **strict**
  mode that does *not* strip reporter page markers or footnote numbers,
  and all 46 passed strictly: no quote in this document silently
  crosses a page break or a footnote marker. The single intended
  elision uses the corpus's ` ... ` convention, and the checker
  requires each fragment to appear **in order**, not merely to appear.
- Verification was re-run reading the quotes back out of the **merged**
  `data/clauses.json`: 46 quotes, 0 failures.
- **Phrase-level search verification: 15 of the 17 case quotes
  returned `count: 1`** on a `cluster_id:<id> AND "<exact phrase>"`
  query against the public search feed. Two returned `count: 0`, and
  both were then probed with sub-phrases rather than left unexplained:
  - *Kirkeby*'s 30-word description of a lis pendens. Its two halves
    each return `count: 1` separately (`"a recorded document giving
    constructive notice that an action has been filed"` and `"right to
    possession of the real property described in the notice"`, and also
    `"an action has been filed affecting title"`, which straddles the
    join). The full sentence is therefore indexed; the index simply
    does not match a phrase that long.
  - *Park 100*'s sentence on re-recording. `"When a lis pendens has
    been expunged"`, `"has been expunged, the claimant"`, and `"may
    record another notice of pendency of action"` each return
    `count: 1`; the tail `"only with leave of court"` — six words, five
    of them function words — returns `count: 0`, as does `"the claimant
    may record another notice"`. The pattern tracks stopword handling
    in the index, not a textual difference: the immediately following
    sentence of the same paragraph ("An order expunging a lis pendens
    is not appealable . . .") returns `count: 1`.
  - A search for a second CourtListener record of *Park 100* found
    none — cluster 2255930 is the only one — so for all three opinions
    the second and third copies are distinct HTTP fetches of one
    CourtListener record under different header sets and URLs, not
    independently sourced texts. That limit is stated here rather than
    worked around; no California-courts PDF exists for opinions of
    these years, and Justia, FindLaw, Google Scholar, and case.law are
    egress-blocked.
- Checked for `{{` leakage in every clause `title` and in every
  citation's `case`/`cite`/`url`/`quote` field — clean. Placeholders
  appear only in clause `body` and `gap` text, and all three
  (`propertyAddress`, `countyName`, `partyName`) are declared fields of
  the document.
- Drafting clauses carry exactly `{id,title,kind,body}`; authority
  clauses exactly `{id,title,kind,status,checkedDate,body,gap,
  citations}` in that order; citations exactly `{case,cite,url,quote}`;
  every authority clause has a non-empty `gap`.
- Corpus-wide validation after merge: both files parse; 5,653 clause
  ids and 673 document ids are unique; every `clauseOrder` entry in
  every document resolves to an existing clause; every `{{placeholder}}`
  in every clause reachable from a document resolves to a field that
  document declares. The only validator complaints across the whole
  corpus are 103 pre-existing legacy documents whose top-level keys are
  ordered `id,title,description,clauseOrder,fields,categories`; the new
  document uses the current
  `id,title,description,categories,clauseOrder,fields` order.
- Duplicate-id check against the full corpus before merge: none for the
  15 clause ids, none for the document id.

## Genuine findings

- **A disclosed structural contrast with the existing § 201.5
  document**: § 201.5's own "next regular payday" rule applies to a
  termination "whether by discharge, layoff, resignation, completion
  of employment for a specified term, or otherwise" — i.e., discharge
  is *included* in its own schedule. § 201.8's text instead expressly
  *excludes* a discharge and a quit from its own next-payday
  definition, routing them to Sections 201/202. This is a genuine,
  verified textual difference between two parallel-seeming statutes,
  disclosed as an observation rather than an assumption about intent.
- **A disclosed payment-method contrast**: § 201.8(c) includes direct
  deposit as an express payment option; § 201.5(c), as already quoted
  in this corpus's existing motion-picture document, does not mention
  direct deposit at all (only mailing or on-site availability).
- **A disclosed structural contrast with § 201.3's own gap-filling
  device**: § 201.3(b)(6) caps its own weekly-default rule at 90
  consecutive calendar days for one client assignment; § 201.8(d)'s
  continuous-employment deeming rule, by contrast, states no
  comparable outer time or gap-length limit — a genuine textual
  absence, disclosed rather than invented.
- **Confirmed § 203 penalty-list treatment**: § 201.8 is separately
  named on Section 203(a)'s list (unlike § 201.7, per the existing
  oil-drilling document) — verified directly against the fetched
  § 203 text rather than assumed from the oil-drilling document's own
  disclosure.
- **A disclosed negative CourtListener result**: a search for case law
  construing § 201.8 itself (terms: "201.8" "events employee" baseball
  venue; and "section 201.8" Labor Code events employee professional
  baseball) returned zero results in both queries — disclosed honestly
  rather than treated as supporting or undermining authority.

## Honest gap(s) disclosed

This document does not resolve how limited a period, or what
"expectation of an ongoing employment relationship," means under the
§ 201.8(a)(1) carve-out (the statute does not further define either
phrase). It does not resolve what county counts as where a
multi-venue events employee "performed labor" for payment-location
purposes. It does not state any outer time or gap-length limit on the
§ 201.8(d) continuous-employment deeming rule, because the statute's
own text states none. It does not independently fetch or characterize
Unemployment Insurance Code § 1251 et seq. or § 1253.8, which
§ 201.8(f) cross-references by name without disturbing; only the
cross-reference itself is quoted. It does not restate Labor Code
§§ 201, 202, 204, or 203(b)'s statute of limitations, which this
corpus addresses in other, dedicated documents.

# Confidentiality & IP, new document: Right to Repair Act — Trade Secret, IP, and Source Code Disclosure Limits Information Sheet

## Why this document

Third processed item of wave 126 (Confidentiality & IP slot, 1×4
pattern). The agent ruled out ten candidates via specific
section-number greps before landing here — UTSA exemplary damages
(§3426.3), CUTSA preemption (§3426.7), DTSA whistleblower notice
(§1833(b)), DMCA §512 notice-and-takedown, trademark
genericide/incontestability (§1065), Labor Code invention-assignment
notice (§§2870-2872), posthumous publicity (Civ. Code §3344.1), false
and standard patent marking (§292/§287(a)), and Labor Code whistleblower
retaliation (§1102.5) were all confirmed already substantively covered.
The chosen topic — California's electronics/appliance Right to Repair
Act (Pub. Resources Code §§42488-42488.3, added by SB 244, operative
July 1, 2024), specifically its statutory limits on the
documentation-and-parts mandate for trade secrets, other intellectual
property, source code, and antitheft security measures — was confirmed
genuinely open: zero prior corpus hits on §42488 in any form before
drafting. The document is explicitly disambiguated from this corpus's
existing, differently-scoped "Right to Repair Act — Information Sheet"
document (Civil Code §§895-945.5, the SB 800 construction-defect
statute), which shares only a common short title.

## What this document covers

11 clauses: 2 drafting (intro, closing acknowledgment) and 9 authority
clauses, citing Pub. Resources Code §42488.1, §42488.2(a)-(b), (c),
(d), (f), (j)(2), (j)(3)(A), (j)(7), (j)(8), (l), §42488.3(a)(1),
(a)(2), (b):

- **`electrepair_legislative_purpose`** — the Act's stated legislative
  intent.
- **`electrepair_documentation_mandate_two_tiers`** — the two
  price-tiered (3-year/7-year) documentation-parts-tools mandate that
  the carve-outs limit.
- **`electrepair_trade_secret_ip_carveout`** — the core clause: no
  requirement to divulge trade secrets or license IP (copyrights or
  patents).
- **`electrepair_source_code_carveout`** — the unqualified source-code
  carve-out.
- **`electrepair_trade_secret_definition_crossreference`** — the
  Act's cross-reference to CUTSA's and the Penal Code's existing
  trade-secret definitions, deliberately not re-quoted here.
- **`electrepair_documentation_and_tool_scope`** — the statutory
  definitions of "documentation" and "tool."
- **`electrepair_antitheft_security_carveout`** — the separate,
  owner-authorization-only antitheft carve-out.
- **`electrepair_scope_and_operative_date`** — the covered-product
  cross-reference and the July 1, 2024 operative date.
- **`electrepair_civil_enforcement`** — government-only suit authority
  and the escalating per-day penalty structure.

  comply" qualifier that qualifies subdivision (c)'s trade-secret/IP
  carve-out — explicitly labeled as the agent's own direct textual

- **A disclosed, deliberate choice not to independently verify a
  cross-referenced statute**: the scope-and-operative-date clause's
  gap discloses that Bus. & Prof. Code §9801(h)-(k), cross-referenced
  by the covered-product definition, was not independently fetched,
  quoted, or verified.
- **A disclosed, deliberate choice not to cite unverified case law**:
  CourtListener's daily quota was already exhausted on the agent's
  test call, and the statute's substantive provisions are barely 21

## Honest gap(s) disclosed

This document does not resolve what quantum of disclosure the
"except as necessary to comply with this section" qualifier requires
in any specific case, does not determine whether any specific
material a manufacturer withholds actually qualifies as a trade
secret, protected intellectual property, or source code, does not
independently verify Bus. & Prof. Code §9801(h)-(k)'s product-category
definitions, does not restate §42488.2(j)(3)(B)'s express product
exclusions (alarm systems, video game consoles, etc.) or the
statute's other definitions (authorized repair provider, part, fair
and reasonable terms), does not take a position on whether a private
right of action exists alongside the government-suit enforcement
mechanism (the statute's own text is silent on that question), and
does not address the separate Civil Code §§895-945.5 construction-
defect statute this corpus documents elsewhere under the same short
title.

## Method

leginfo.legislature.ca.gov, each fetched twice by the research agent
with two distinct User-Agent strings, confirmed byte-identical. The
enacted bill text (SB 244, 2023-2024) was also fetched as a
cross-check of the codified sections. CourtListener's daily quota was
already exhausted (HTTP 429) on the agent's test call; no case law was

confirmed by the research agent as exact, contiguous substrings.

Independently re-verified during integration review via a

sections — all 14 citation instances confirmed clean on direct
normalized-whitespace substring match, with no fallback needed. The
legislative-purpose statement, both documentation-mandate price tiers,
the trade-secret/IP carve-out, the source-code carve-out, the
trade-secret cross-reference, the documentation/tool definitions, the
antitheft carve-out, the covered-product definition and operative

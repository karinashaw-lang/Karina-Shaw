# Ending employment, new document: At-Will Employment Presumption and Implied-in-Fact Contract Limits on Termination (Labor Code § 2922; Guz v. Bechtel) — Information Sheet

## Why this document

Processed item of wave 156 (Ending employment slot, continuing the
round-robin with waves 146/148/150/152/154). Read all 81 existing
"Ending employment" document titles/ids in `data/documents.json`
before choosing a topic, and grepped both data files for candidate
subjects. Two candidates suggested by the task brief were confirmed
already covered and ruled out: the Labor Commissioner wage-claim
(Berman hearing) procedure (`dlse_wage_claim_info_sheet`) and
constructive discharge under Turner v. Anheuser-Busch
(`constructive_discharge_info_sheet`). Statute-only alternatives were

`california_paid_sick_leave_info_sheet`; wage-action fee/interest
statutes §§ 218.5/218.6 — open, but set aside once the case-law path
recovered).

The chosen topic — the Labor Code section 2922 at-will presumption and
the implied-in-fact-contract doctrine that displaces it (Guz v. Bechtel
National, Inc. (2000) 24 Cal.4th 317; Foley v. Interactive Data Corp.
(1988) 47 Cal.3d 654) — was confirmed genuinely open. The corpus had
zero hits for "Guz," "Cotran," or "covenant of good faith," one
passing mention each of Foley and Pugh (in an unrelated idea-submission
clause), and section 2922 itself was quoted only as a one-sentence
baseline in the wrongful-termination-public-policy sheet (Ending
employment) and the fixed-term-contract sheet (Hiring). Neither
existing document addresses the implied-in-fact contract, the Foley
factors, the weight of longevity/raises/promotions, handbook
disclaimers, or the implied covenant's limits — which are the
practical questions an employee or employer actually faces when a
separation is challenged as a breach of an unwritten job-security
understanding.

## What this document covers

8 clauses: 1 drafting (applicability note) and 7 authority clauses,
citing Cal. Lab. Code § 2922, Guz v. Bechtel National, Inc. (2000) 24
Cal.4th 317 (25 quotations), and Foley v. Interactive Data Corp. (1988)
47 Cal.3d 654 (4 quotations):

- **`atwillguz_declaration`** (drafting) — applicability note naming
  the employee, employer, start date, and separation date while
  disclaiming any determination that an implied agreement existed.
- **`atwillguz_statutory_presumption`** — § 2922's text; Guz's
  description of at-will employment ("at any time without cause," no
  procedure but notice); the "a fortiori" sentence on the employer's
  latitude; the presumption is "strong" but "subject to several
  limitations."
- **`atwillguz_freedom_to_contract_around_default`** — § 2922 yields
  where the parties "have reached no other understanding"; any
  otherwise-lawful limit may be agreed; parties may define causes and
  procedures; "good cause" as Guz quoted it from Scott.
- **`atwillguz_implied_in_fact_contract_and_foley_factors`** — the
  agreement "may be implied in fact" (Guz); the Pugh/Foley factors
  quoted from Foley itself; Foley's statement that § 2922 "does not
  diminish the force" of enforceable expectations about the grounds or
  manner of termination.
- **`atwillguz_totality_not_factors_in_a_bag`** — Guz's "shaken
  together in a bag" clarification; "actual understanding"; the
  "tendency in reason" test; conflicting inferences = question of
  fact; summary judgment where undisputed facts negate the contract.
- **`atwillguz_longevity_raises_promotions_alone_insufficient`** —
  Guz's holding that longevity, raises, promotions, and good reviews
  "cannot alone" create an implied contract; "their own rewards"; the
  counter-rule that long service is "not necessarily irrelevant";
  Foley's own "six years and nine months is sufficient time" sentence.
- **`atwillguz_handbook_disclaimers_and_personnel_policies`** — an
  express disclaimer satisfies the employer's initial burden but does
  not necessarily foreclose proof of an implied agreement; disclaimers
  "must be taken into account"; written policies as "an important
  source of implied-contract evidence"; the "strong inference" of
  reliance on disseminated handbooks; Guz quoting Foley on reasonable
  reliance; Bechtel's "absolute right" to eliminate the work unit.
- **`atwillguz_implied_covenant_no_independent_limit_no_tort`** — the
  covenant adds no limits beyond the contract; "no expectation,
  protectible by law"; policies that became implied terms are enforced
  as contract; "no tort of 'bad faith breach'"; Foley's holding that
  tort remedies are unavailable.

## Method

Fetched Cal. Lab. Code § 2922 (and §§ 2924-2925, ultimately unused)
twice each from leginfo.legislature.ca.gov via curl with two distinct
User-Agent strings ("Groundtruth-verifier/1.0 (wave156 endempl)" and a
Chrome-style UA); operative text identical.

Case law: every non-CourtListener host was unreachable through the
egress proxy — CONNECT refused for scocal.stanford.edu,
scholar.google.com, static.case.law, cite.case.law, anylaw, leagle,
casemine, web.archive.org, and Wikipedia; HTTP 403 (bot block, with
either User-Agent) from law.justia.com and caselaw.findlaw.com; HTTP 410
from casetext; HTTP 202 empty bot-challenge from the CourtListener web
page; HTTP 401 from the REST API. CourtListener's MCP `search` found
Guz (cluster 2569285; lead opinion id 9785497) before the shared
125/day quota was exhausted; the Foley (1170824), Cotran (1125361), and
Pugh (2149787) opinion ids were then recovered from the citation
hyperlinks embedded in Guz's `html_with_citations` text via
`search_document`. Guz chunks 3-8 and 9-14 and Foley chunks 9-14 and 18
(8,000-character windows) were retrieved via `read_document`; each
range was then retrieved a second time in a separate call, and the two
copies were byte-identical (`cmp`). The two-independent-paths
requirement was therefore satisfied via two separate retrievals of one
underlying database, plus `search_document` literal-phrase lookups on
Guz ("totality of the circumstances," "shaken together in a bag") each
returning the expected single position — disclosed honestly as one
host queried twice rather than genuinely separate hosting
infrastructure. Cotran could not be retrieved at all (uncached on the
MCP server; every attempt returned 429 with 75-95 minutes remaining on
the daily window), and the agent waited in-turn with sleep loops
across roughly two hours before finalizing without it.

All 31 citation-quote instances across the 7 authority clauses were
programmatically verified by a Python script asserting each quote is
an exact contiguous substring of both the A-series and B-series copy
of its source after the disclosed normalizations (whitespace collapse;
removal of `<page-number>` / star-pagination markup and other HTML
tags; HTML entity unescaping; nbsp → space). First run (before the
second retrievals and with a stricter every-copy rule): 22/29
substring hits, the 7 non-hits being quotes whose source chunks were
not yet on disk. Final run: 31/31.

## WHAT WE'RE BUILDING (in one paragraph)

A paid, California-only, self-help legal document product. Users
answer a short interview, see every clause with its source, quote, and
check date — for free, before paying — then pay to download a stamped
document backed by a public verification page. Documents stay current
as law changes (the subscription). Trackers and reminders keep users
coming back after the document is signed (the glue). Everything a user
sees must be literally true and generated from real data. Everything a
user sees must be literally true and generated from real data.

**The one-liner:** AI can write you a contract. We can prove ours.

**The 60-second demo every user must experience:**

1. Answer a short interview (form an LLC, or make a roommate agreement).
2. See every clause with its source, quote, and check date — before paying.
3. Pay, download — plus the official filing form already filled in.
4. Get a reminder before a real deadline (e.g., Statement of Information).
5. Months later: the law changed under your document; click once, it's updated.

## THE LAUNCH SET (two tracks)

**Track A — business lifecycle (depth, the trust flagship).** The
corpus already has ~741 documents. A document becomes sellable the day
it clears the verifier — never before. Release in waves:

- **Wave 1 — Form it (launch):** the 7 verified single-member CA LLC
  formation documents.
- **Wave 2 — Run it:** multi-member operating agreement, annual
  minutes, written consents, company resolutions, amendment of
  articles, registered agent change, DBA kit. **Grow it:** independent
  contractor agreement, client service agreement, employment offer
  letter, NDA (these feed the "new client" repeat loop).
- **Wave 3 — Close it:** dissolution/cancellation documents, final
  checklist.

**Track B — everyday documents (volume, impulse pricing ~$5–9
placeholder).** Short interviews (≤6 questions, facts only). Launch
list: roommate agreement, promissory note (loan between friends), bill
of sale, sublease, pet agreement, demand letter, NDA, freelance service
agreement. Same proof layer as Track A — sources, quotes, check dates —
the proof is the brand and never gets cut.

Corpus documents outside these tracks stay in the app but are not
featured, indexed, or purchasable until they join a track and clear
verification.

## GLOBAL RULES (never break these)

1. **Inform, never advise.** No copy may recommend a legal course of
   action, assess a user's position, or claim legal sufficiency,
   enforceability, or outcomes.
2. **Free before payment:** browsing, search, interview, live preview,
   sourcing badges, and the contract reader — no account required. Pay
   only at export/save/sign.
3. **Honest verification.** Never mark a clause Verified or change a
   checkedDate without re-checking the primary source. Every citation
   records sourceClass (primary-official, court-opinion, agency,
   mirror, secondary). A mirror is not primary.
4. **Gates before every commit:** `python validate_corpus.py`, `node
   regression.js`, `python merge_guard.py`, plus newer gates. No commit
   on failure.
5. **Never touch config.js contents or commit secrets.**
6. **Never auto-edit legal clause text.** Write patch files, show the
   diff, wait for owner approval.
7. **Never run destructive or irreversible actions** (DROP, DELETE,
   force-push, deploy, publish, live-mode Stripe, real charges) without
   showing the exact action and waiting for a yes.
8. **Never store card data.** Stripe ids only. Test mode only until the
   owner flips live mode.
9. **All paywall enforcement is server-side.** Client gating is
   cosmetic.
10. **Correctness beats breadth.** Unverifiable content is labeled or
    removed, never shipped as Verified.
11. **Agent work goes on child branches/worktrees.** Never commit
    directly to the main working branch.
12. **Every public number or claim is generated from data**, never
    typed by hand.
13. **Ambiguity:** choose the most conservative option, log it in
    DECISIONS.md, continue.
14. **After each step**, update BUILD_STATUS truthfully and report:
    step id, files changed, checks/results, skips and why, next step.
    Keep it short.
15. **Clerical self-help only.** We may compute, fill, and assemble
    official forms from user facts and sourced rules (the TurboTax
    ground). We never pick which form applies or judge applicability.
16. **Owner decision (accepted risk, in DECISIONS.md):** no attorney
    review, no lawyers, no E&O insurance. Pure self-serve software; the
    design-level protections (copy lint, honest labels, disclaimers)
    carry the load.

## PART 1: MAKE THE TRUST CLAIM TRUE

### STEP 1: Baseline (no code changes)

Re-measure everything: documents, clauses, citations, tiers, verifier
coverage, open findings by severity, unverified clauses,
mirror/secondary-reliant clauses. Write MVP_BASELINE.md; flag
mismatches with BUILD_STATUS.

### STEP 2: Honest labels and tiers

1. Three visible verification levels on every clause: **Cited**
   (citation, quote, check date), **Verified** (primary-source check
   passed), **Adversarially checked** (survived the verifier, no open
   findings). Badge shows level, check date, one-line plain
   explanation.
2. Add sourceClass to all citations (backfill by URL domain). List
   every mirror/secondary clause in verifier/NON_PRIMARY_SOURCES.md
   (start with the 9 small-claims CCP sections); queue for primary
   re-verification.
3. Redefine tiers: Tier A needs ≥1 authority clause, all authority
   clauses adversarially checked with zero open findings, a golden
   fixture per branch, fresh check dates, accessibility pass. Documents
   with no authority clauses: label "Drafting only (no legal authority
   claimed)"; never Tier A.
4. Update methodology.html, tiering.py, validate_corpus.py, the picker,
   and the sourcing UI. Recompute tiers.
5. Acceptance: nothing shows a level it hasn't earned.

### STEP 3: Mechanical controls

1. Pre-commit/pre-push hook + CI check blocking agent-session commits
   to the main working branch.
2. CI check: any legal-text change without an approved patch id in the
   commit message fails.
3. Acceptance: a deliberately violating test commit is blocked.

### STEP 4: Lock the launch set (owner decision — propose, then WAIT)

1. Launch set = the two tracks above. Track A in verification waves;
   Track B = the 8 everyday documents with ≤6-question interviews
   (facts only, no branching advice trees) and impulse pricing.
2. Write research files for any launch-set document lacking them; the
   121-document backlog is deferred.
3. WAIT for owner approval of the final list. Only approved documents
   are published, promoted, or sold.

### STEP 5: Verify the launch set

1. Estimate verifier cost/time for every authority clause in both
   tracks (Track A in waves — Wave 1 first, Waves 2–3 queue right
   after), plus drafting-clause checks for advisory language, internal
   contradiction, jurisdiction disclosure. WAIT for approval, then run
   in parallel batches with a resume file.
2. Triage blockers → majors → minors. For each blocker/major: show the
   clause, evidence, patch. Apply only after owner approval, re-verify
   against primary source, update checkedDate, add a changelog.html
   entry. (Includes the 5 wedge patch proposals awaiting owner
   approval.)
3. Anything unconfirmable: labeled unverified; document excluded or
   clearly labeled.
4. Produce verifier/LAUNCH_SET_REPORT.md (finding rates by category,
   severity, failure type).
5. Acceptance: zero open blockers/majors in the launch set; every
   launch-set authority clause has a recorded result.

## PART 2: PAYMENTS AND SERVER-SIDE DELIVERY

### STEP 6: Pricing (owner decision — propose, then WAIT)

1. Model: per-document one-time purchase + a subscription whose only
   job is keeping documents current (law-change alerts + one-click
   updates). No credits or metering in MVP.
2. 30-day money-back guarantee as a marketed feature; exact refund copy
   flagged for owner approval.
3. Paywalled: final PDF, .docx, filled official form, sources appendix.
   Free: interview, preview, sourcing badges, contract reader, update
   alerts.
4. Impulse tier for Track B (~$5–9 placeholder; NDA etc. ~$9–15): the
   comparison is not "free vs. paid" but "free but uncertain" vs.
   "trivial price, verified, done in 3 minutes." Race to trivial, not
   to zero.
5. Checkout extras (see Step 14A): order bumps and moment bundles,
   placeholders in config/pricing.json.
6. All price points are placeholders in config/pricing.json. WAIT for
   owner decision.

### STEP 7: Entitlements and server-side release

1. Supabase migrations (never manual SQL): confirm/extend orders and
   order_items; add entitlements (user_id, type, document_id nullable,
   source order/subscription id, status, starts_at, ends_at). RLS
   scoped to auth.uid(), with adversarial tests for every table (second
   user cannot select/insert/update/delete). Run get_advisors after
   each migration.
2. Edge function release-document: verifies session + entitlement,
   renders final PDF/.docx server-side from the same clause set and
   template as the client, returns a signed short-lived URL. Log to
   audit_log: document id, corpus version, clause ids + check dates,
   timestamp, output hash. Never log field values.
3. Server output must pass golden text-parity tests against the client
   preview.
4. Guest checkout with email only (magic link); purchase claimed on
   first sign-in.
5. Remove or watermark client-side print/JSON/text paths for paid
   documents.
6. Redeploy release-document to cover the full launch bundle (currently
   scoped to the 3-document test bundle) — carried-in open item.
7. Acceptance: no entitlement → clear "purchase required"; entitlement
   → the file; manual client-bypass checklist run and recorded.

### STEP 8: Stripe (test mode only)

1. Stripe Checkout through an edge function; products and prices
   generated from config/pricing.json.
2. Webhooks with signature verification + idempotency (stored event
   ids; replays are no-ops). Handle: checkout.session.completed,
   invoice.paid, invoice.payment_failed,
   customer.subscription.updated/deleted, charge.refunded,
   charge.dispute.created.
3. Refunds: 30-day guarantee, admin refund action, automatic
   entitlement revocation. Disputes flag the account and revoke
   entitlement pending review.
4. Customer portal for subscription management, receipts, payment
   methods; receipts/order history/invoice export in the account area.
5. California automatic-renewal requirements (statute from
   leginfo.legislature.ca.gov, cited in DECISIONS.md): clear terms
   before purchase, affirmative consent, acknowledgment email,
   cancellation as easy as sign-up. Renewal copy strictly factual (copy
   lint enforced).
6. Evaluate Stripe Tax; record sales-tax treatment as an accountant
   question in DECISIONS.md. Never assert tax conclusions.
7. Abuse controls: rate limits on checkout/release, per-entitlement
   download limits, anomaly alerts.
8. Acceptance: test-mode end-to-end passes for purchase, failed
   payment, refund, renewal, cancellation, duplicate webhook replay,
   dispute, unauthorized release.

## PART 3: WHAT MAKES THIS NOT LEGALZOOM

### STEP 9: Show-your-work document view

1. Live document beside a sources panel: selecting a clause shows

2. Optional plain-language "why this clause is here" line per clause —
   advice-free, copy-lint checked. If missing, show nothing; never
   invent.
3. Acceptance: every launch-set clause renders in both layouts;
   accessibility passes.

### STEP 10: Verification stamp and public verify page

1. Every paid export carries a short document ID + footer: "Sources
   and check dates for this document: [URL]".
2. /verify/ shows: document title, generated-on date, corpus version,
   every clause id with level + check date, and a status line ("No
   clause used in this document has changed since it was generated" or
   the changed-clause list with diffs). Facts only; never say whether
   the user must act.
3. Store only: id, document id, corpus version hash, clause ids + check
   dates, timestamp, output hash. Unguessable ids, rate limiting.
   Adversarial test proving the page/API exposes no user data and
   cannot be enumerated.
4. Acceptance: stamp on every export; page works fresh and after a
   clause change; privacy test passes.

### STEP 11: Free contract reader as the front door

1. Reachable without an account from home + SEO pages. Output stays
   "law this passage resembles" with sources and check dates; "no close
   match found" below threshold; never assesses the contract.
2. Clear note on what it does and doesn't do; copy lint over all
   wording.
3. Re-run the 55-case evaluation; no regression from 78% top-1 / 98%
   top-3.
4. Pasted text never sent to any server unless the owner approves in
   writing.
5. The AI-crowd funnel: position it as the second opinion for
   ChatGPT-drafted contracts — "got a contract from AI? see which
   California laws it matches, with sources and check dates. Free."
   Facts only; never claim AI is wrong in general.
6. Acceptance: mobile-usable; accessibility passes; the framing passes
   copy lint.

### STEP 12: Documents that stay alive (the subscription)

1. Drift detection → email via edge function for opted-in users. Email
   lists the changed clause + links the diff. Facts only. Alerts are
   free for purchased documents — they are the trust promise.
2. One-click update for subscribers: re-renders from the current corpus
   with saved field values preserved, stored as a new version (old
   version intact with original check dates). Update screen shows the
   diff first — what changed, source, date. Applying + re-exporting
   requires a subscription (or repurchase). Server-enforced,
   bypass-tested.
3. Dashboard health line: "X of your Y documents are current; Z updates
   available" — factual only.
4. Acceptance: a seeded clause change produces exactly one alert to the
   right users; subscriber updates in one click with values preserved;
   free user gets server-side paywall; golden parity passes.

### STEP 13: Live trust page

1. /trust, rebuilt in CI: launch-set documents and clauses, share at
   each verification level, last law-change check, defects found/fixed
   with changelog links, link to methodology.html.
2. CI fails the build if page text and data disagree.
3. Acceptance: numbers match the data; no legal-sufficiency claim.

### STEP 14: Paid experience and home page

The largest buyer segment never subscribes. They buy the FEELING of

1. Officialness sells the purchase. COPY GUARDRAIL: say "verified,"
   "sourced," "professional" — NEVER "official," "government,"
   "court-approved," or anything implying state endorsement.
2. Order bumps at checkout (placeholders in config/pricing.json): e-sign

   sourcing standards.
3. Moment bundles: sell the whole life moment — roommate agreement +
   security deposit receipt + move-in checklist; bill of sale +
   title-transfer sheet + odometer statement. One impulse-tier bundle
   price; contents only from verified launch-set documents.
4. The 30-day guarantee displayed at the pay button.
5. The signature loop is the acquisition channel: every shared document
   shows the counterparty what this is + a "make your own" link. Every

6. Analytics: order-bump take rate, bundle take rate, checkout

7. Acceptance: bumps/bundles render server-side with correct

### STEP 15: Filing sheets + we fill the official form (the wow feature)

1. Filing instruction sheets for every launch-set document that gets

2. Form-fill: stamp interview answers into the OFFICIAL California form

4. Acceptance: every sheet item sourced + dated; filled SC-100 passes
   golden parity; no advisory wording.

### STEP 16: Statement of Information reminder

1. At formation purchase, offer an opt-in SoI reminder. Collect only:

2. Compute the due window from entity type + formation date; verify the

3. Edge function sends one reminder ~60 days before and one ~14 days

4. Acceptance: seeded due dates produce exactly the right emails;

### STEP 17: The hook — signature/viewed notifications

1. Extend share-view/share-sign: email the sender when the recipient

3. Privacy: recipient identity is whatever the sender entered; no

4. Acceptance: a seeded share triggers exactly one viewed + one signed

1. /dashboard: your documents (health status from Step 12), upcoming

### STEP 18A: Who-owes-me tracker (glue)

1. When a purchased document contains a payment schedule (promissory

2. The payee marks payments received; dashboard shows

3. Optional polite reminders to the payer ("payment 3 of 6 for

2. Opt-in reminders at 60/30/7 days. Facts only: what the date is, where

5. Acceptance: two seeded roommates can split expenses, mark payments,

### STEP 19A: Cease-and-desist shield

A C&D comes from one of five places. Prevent each:

1. **UPL complaint (#1 trigger).** (a) Copy lint sweeps EVERY
   user-facing string on every page, email, and export in CI — full-site,
   not just new text. (b) The disclaimer is one canonical string with CI
   placement tests everywhere a document is previewed, bought, or
   exported. (c) Banned words enforced in copy AND UI labels: no
   "review," "recommend," "protect your rights," "legal advice,"
   "attorney," "we suggest," "you should." (d) The product never picks
   the user's document or judges applicability — search-first discovery
   means the user always picks. (e) Keep a compliance evidence file
   (lint configs, CI runs, placements) so any complaint gets answered
   with documentation.
2. **Auto-renewal claims (biggest actual exposure — CA Automatic
   Renewal Law, B&P §17600+).** (a) Renewal terms in plain language
   immediately before purchase. (b) Separate affirmative consent
   checkbox, never pre-checked. (c) Confirmation email restating terms
   + how to cancel. (d) Cancellation in ≤2 clicks from the dashboard,
   immediate, no dark patterns. (e) Reminder email before any annual
   renewal charge. (f) The 30-day guarantee honored without friction —
   a refunded complainer doesn't write demand letters. CI test: cancel
   path + consent checkbox work on every build.
3. **Trademark.** (a) OWNER TASK before launch: clear the product name
   and domain (USPTO + CA SOS + web search). (b) Never use competitor
   marks in logos/ads/metadata implying affiliation. (c) Teardown pages
   use competitor/AI names as factual nominative use only — no logos,
   no "better than" claims, only what verifier data literally shows.
4. **Copyright.** (a) Corpus from primary sources + our own drafting
   only (official CA Judicial Council forms like SC-100 are safe to
   fill). (b) Hard rule in DECISIONS.md: no competitor text in the
   corpus, no scraping competitor sites. (c) Clause provenance (source +
   check date) doubles as the copyright defense.
5. **Claim hygiene (false advertising, B&P §17200/17500).** CI fails the
   build if any marketing claim ("verified," "current," "X clauses
   checked") can't be traced to corpus or verifier output. No "as good
   as a lawyer," no outcome claims, no enforceability claims, ever.
6. **Complaint intake + response runbook.** (a) Real monitored contact
   email on legal pages (legal@ placeholder until supplied). (b)
   One-page C&D runbook: acknowledge in 48 hours, take down or banner
   challenged content while evaluated, changelog entry, log everything,
   never argue publicly. (c) Owner decision point: a real C&D is the
   one moment to decide whether to consult a lawyer — the product has
   none, the business response is the owner's call. (d) Form the
   business entity (LLC, through the product itself — dogfooding) to
   separate business liability from personal assets.

Acceptance: full-site lint clean; ARL flows pass CI; trademark
clearance documented; C&D runbook written; compliance evidence file
exists.

Click into any paragraph to edit it. Editing a verified clause flags its badge below — the citation was checked against the original wording, not whatever you change it to.

Signature

A plain visual signature kept with this document, on this device — draw it below or just type your name. This isn't a certified e-signature service, and doesn't determine whether or how a signature here is legally binding.

Groundtruth v1 demo · every "Verified" badge links to a real, checked source — click one to see it.

Read a contract

Paste in a contract, offer letter, or agreement someone sent you. Each passage is compared against Groundtruth's clause library — where it resembles something already checked, you'll see the law it rests on; where it doesn't, that's shown plainly too. This describes what the text resembles. It doesn't assess your contract or tell you what to do about it.

Account

Signing in is optional. Nothing about generating or saving a document requires it — this only adds syncing your saved documents to other devices.

Inbox

Every date typed into a saved document, gathered in one place — a start date, a notice date, an expiration date. This reports what's there and how far away it is. It doesn't flag anything as urgent or say what to do about it.

Promises

A quick, informal commitment made or received — not a document, just something worth not forgetting. Typed in here, kept on this device.

Relationships

Every name typed into a saved document, gathered by who it is — so a company, landlord, employee, or counterparty who shows up in more than one place is easy to see, together.

My documents

Every document you generate is saved here automatically, in your browser only — nothing is sent anywhere. Use it to check whether a value you typed the same way across documents (a company name, a restriction period, a person's name) actually matches everywhere it appears.
